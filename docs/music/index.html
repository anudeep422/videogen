How it works

Synthesised Indian instruments (veena, sitar, tanpura, santoor, bansuri, harmonium, tabla, mridangam, ghatam, dholak, gajjalu, manjira, bells, singing bowls, marimba, guitar, harp) are calculated by simulating the physics of each instrument, with no recordings, and they work offline. Music follows real structure: an alap intro, the tala cycle (Teentaal, Keherwa, Dadra, Adi tala), gamakas and meend slides, a climax with fills, and a tihai ending on Sa. Recorded instrument samples (FluidR3 SoundFont, MIT licence) need the internet the first time. The same settings and tune number always give the same music.
